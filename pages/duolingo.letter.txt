label: A letter to the Duolingo team
greeting: Dear Duolingo team,

My first design job started in college. My classmates at National Taiwan University were building PaGamO, a game where middle and high school students conquer territory on a map by answering questions from their schoolwork. Its name is Taiwanese for "play the game and learn." It taught me that a good game can make people want to learn.

Since then, I've spent most of my career in healthcare, where the stakes are different but the question is the same: what keeps someone coming back, long after the novelty wears off?

At Fay, daily goals ran on a prize draw. It lifted DAU, but my research showed people stay for their dietitian. So I redesigned goals around that relationship, with progress people can see. I also took on the screen providers use most, one design, product and engineering had put off for years, and set a strict limit on what deserves their attention.

I care about the small things too: an onboarding animation that sets a calm pace, and a celebration I fought to keep in the release.

Duolingo is where I started: learning and play, together. I'd love to help you keep people learning for years.

closing: Thanks for reading.
