Designing for what providers need next

From one nudge to a notification system, built in code.

The provider home with To-dos open from the bell: chart notes before they lock, license details before removal, and a client to follow up with.

  • Fay NutritionWorkshop

    Our provider team knew every problem by heart. One afternoon turned them into a plan.

    The provider team asked for help with onboarding, activation and growing a practice. They knew the problems, but their days went to answering questions and fixing things by hand. My design colleague Tori and I knew what the product could do. We printed every email and screen a new provider sees, from the first call to the first payout, and turned each frustration into an idea: some we could ship now, some that fit work already planned. The room went from “so much is broken” to “we can do this now, and that next.”
